# Community conduct

Be respectful, specific and constructive. Critique ideas and code, not people's identities.
Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing and publication of private information are not
welcome in project issues, pull requests or discussions. Do not post unsolicited promotion.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, close discussions, or restrict participation.
Report conduct concerns privately to jeff@hypelab.digital with a link and a short explanation;
include only information necessary to investigate. Reports will be handled discreetly, with
information shared only as necessary to address the report. No guaranteed response time is promised.
