# Support

Cast is a developer preview, maintained on a best-effort basis. For reproducible bugs or
feature requests, use [GitHub issues](https://github.com/jeffweisbein/cast/issues).
Include your Cast version/source revision, macOS/iOS and gateway versions, expected and
observed behavior, and minimal reproduction steps. Redact screenshots and logs first.
Do not attach tokens, setup codes, private transcripts, machine addresses or identity files.

For vulnerabilities, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), not a public issue.
There is no hosted Cast service or paid support commitment. Gateway account, model-provider
and infrastructure issues belong to the operator of that service.
